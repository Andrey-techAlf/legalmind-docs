1. The Service and Who Can Use It

4. Fair Use and Technical Limits

What “unlimited” means. Premium has no fixed number of chats or document reviews and no charge per chat or per review. It is meant for normal personal use, and the limits below, which are set well above normal personal use, still apply. They keep the Service fast and fair for everyone and stop automated abuse.

  • Document size. One document review accepts a document of up to 40 pages and 300,000 characters of text. For PDFs, scans and photos, a page is a page of the document; for Word and text files and pasted text, the App lays the text out on standard A4 pages (in English, roughly 2,000 to 3,700 characters per page; fewer for Chinese, Japanese or Korean). Split a longer document and review it in parts.
  • Weekly review pages (Premium). Premium includes up to 200 pages of document review in any rolling 7-day period. Our server counts these pages, and only reviews that finish successfully count: the pages of a review are held while it runs and released if it fails or is canceled. The pages of each review become available again 7 days after it finished. If a document doesn’t fit, the App tells you how many pages you have left and when you can review it. This limit does not affect chatting.
  • Chat messages. One chat message can contain up to 12,000 characters of typed or pasted text; attach longer text as a document. The AI sees up to 4 of the photos or scanned pages in a message as images; for the others, it reads only the text the App recognizes in them. In the chat, the AI may read only part of a very long document or of several documents sent together, and in a long conversation it may no longer see documents from earlier messages. A document review reads the whole document within the limits above.
  • Protection against abuse. We also use automatic limits set far above normal personal use: on how many requests a device can send per minute and per day, and on how many document reviews can run at the same time or start within 24 hours. If you reach one, the App tells you, and you can try again later.
