Privacy Policy – Legal Mind

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Previous versions: October 7, 2026, October 1, 2026, May 28, 2026, December 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Andrei Alferchyk, an individual developer (“we”, “us”, “our”), collects, uses and shares information when you use the Legal Mind mobile app (the “App”) and the online service it connects to (together, the “Service”). Our Terms of Use also apply.

What changed in this version

  • This Privacy Policy is now also published in the other languages of the App.
  • “Location” now says that the App keeps the country or its region, not “the country or state”, because the App now offers regions of other countries too.
  • Nothing else in it has changed.

The short version

  • No account. You don’t sign up, and we don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your cases are stored on your device, encrypted. We don’t keep your case history; our server keeps answers and review results only briefly (see below).
  • To answer you, the App sends your messages, the text of your documents and the photos you attach to our server and to our AI providers: third-party providers of AI language models, reached through an AI routing service. They use it to answer you, and their terms for business customers don’t allow them to train AI models on it.
  • Our server keeps an encrypted copy of each answer for about 24 hours and of each review result for about 72 hours, then deletes it.
  • When you accept our Terms of Use, the App keeps a record of it on your device, and our usage analytics note which version you accepted and when. Neither contains your content.
  • We use usage analytics (Mixpanel) and a subscription service (Adapty). They never receive your chats or documents.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Conversations with an AI are not protected by attorney–client privilege.

2. What We Collect

  • What you send. What you type or dictate (as text), the text of documents you attach or have reviewed, and photos and scanned pages you attach to a message. The App reads the text in your photos and scans on your device and sends that text too. So the AI can follow your case, each request also carries context from that case: earlier messages, a summary of the conversation, your documents’ names and text, review results, drafted letters (including names they contain, such as a recipient’s), the case title and the jurisdiction you chose.
  • Device identifier and status. A random device ID that the App creates and keeps in your device’s keychain (it stays if you reinstall the App or use “Delete all data”, and it may move to a new device restored from a backup), and an access token. Linked to it, our server keeps how much of your free allowance you have used, your Premium status and its end date, offers you have used, and the original transaction ID of your Apple subscription. We never receive your Apple Account details or payment card.
  • Records of acceptance. When you tap the button that accepts our Terms of Use, while setting up the App or on the screen that tells you we’ve updated them, the App records which versions of the Terms of Use and this policy were shown to you, the screen and the button you used, a fingerprint of the exact text shown above the button, the date and time, the app version and build, your device model, iOS version, language and time zone. This record is kept on your device, in its keychain, so it stays if you reinstall the App or use “Delete all data”; the versions, the date and the screen are also sent to our usage analytics (Mixpanel). It contains nothing you wrote or uploaded. We keep it so that we can show what was agreed if there is a dispute.
  • Technical information. Each request carries your app version, iOS version and your device’s language and time zone settings. Our server records, without any content: the time, the type of request, the AI model used, its size and cost, the app version, a coded case ID, error codes, a guess of the language, and fingerprints (hashes) of requests and reviewed documents, used to detect repeats.
  • IP address. Our server and our network provider see the IP address of each request.
  • Usage analytics (Mixpanel). An analytics ID, kept in your device’s keychain so that it stays if you reinstall the App or use “Delete all data”. On first launch the App takes it from your device’s identifier for vendor, an ID that Apple gives to all apps from the same developer on that device. With it, events about how you use the App: for example, screens and features you open, how long a message is (never the text itself) and whether it had an attachment, a document’s type and page count, a review’s score and verdict, whether you copied or shared a letter, your answers to onboarding questions, the jurisdiction you choose, when you accepted the Terms of Use and which version, and when the paywall was shown and what you bought, at what price. Mixpanel also receives your device model, iOS and app version, language and region settings, and whether you installed the App from an Apple Search Ads campaign (and which one). Mixpanel uses your IP address to estimate your approximate location (city, region and country).
  • Purchases (Apple and Adapty). Apple processes payments. Adapty receives your purchases and subscription status from the App Store, together with an Adapty profile ID, basic device information and your IP address.
  • Location, only if you ask. When you tap “Use my current location”, your device finds where you are and Apple’s location service names the place. The App keeps only the country or its region (for example, a US state or a Canadian province), and we never receive your coordinates.
  • Voice. Apple’s speech recognition turns your voice into text on your device where supported, and otherwise on Apple’s servers. We receive only the text.

What we don’t collect: your name, email address or phone number (unless you type them into a message, a document contains them or you email us), your contacts, your precise location or your advertising identifier.

3. Your Content and Our AI Providers

Legal Mind’s answers, reviews and letters are written by external AI models from third-party providers of AI language models. To produce them, the App sends what is described under “What you send” in section 2 to our server, which passes it on as follows:

  • The main AI model writes chat answers, letters and document reviews; if it is unavailable, another model made by the same company does. It sees up to 4 of the photos or scanned pages in each message as images; for the others, it receives the text the App recognizes in them. If that company’s own service is unavailable, the request may go to another company that hosts the same models (for example, a large cloud provider), under that company’s own business terms.
  • A second, smaller AI model does small helper tasks: naming a case, summarizing a long conversation (if it fails or its summary stays too long, the main AI model does this instead, only on its own company’s service, never on a backup host) and writing the short progress lines you see while an answer is prepared. For these it sees parts of your conversation, including document names, the beginning of your documents and the text the App reads from your photos and scans, but not the images themselves. Its provider may keep content that its abuse systems flag for up to 30 days to check it.
  • An AI routing service connects us to these models. With each request it receives the content above, a coded identifier derived from your device ID (not the ID itself) and, for chat requests, a coded case ID, which help prevent abuse.

These providers may use what we send only to provide their service to us, and we use only providers whose business terms protect it in line with this policy. Under their terms for business customers, they don’t use it to train their AI models, and we don’t either. The company that makes the main AI model deletes what it receives within 30 days, unless it is flagged for violating that company’s usage policy, in which case it may keep it for up to 2 years, or longer where the law requires. When a request goes to another company that hosts the same models, that company handles it under its own business terms. The AI routing service does not store the content of requests, because we have not turned on its optional logging.

Sending your content to these providers is how the App answers you, so it happens whenever you send a message, start a review or ask for a letter. You agree to it when you accept our Terms of Use in the App. If you don’t want your content processed this way, don’t send it.

We don’t automatically remove names or other personal details from what you send. Share only what your question needs; before uploading a document, you can black out details such as Social Security or other national ID numbers, or account numbers.

4. How Long We Keep Information

  • On your device: your cases, chats, documents, reviews and letters, encrypted, until you delete them. Letters drafted in a case you delete are no longer shown in the App and stay on your device until you use Settings → Delete all data. Your device’s own backups (for example, iCloud Backup) may include them. Temporary copies made while you import or share a file, and copies of recent answers and review results in the App’s temporary network cache, are protected by your device’s encryption and are removed by the App or by iOS, or when you use Delete all data.
  • Answers and drafted letters on our server: encrypted, for about 24 hours, so you still get an answer that was interrupted, for example because you closed the App.
  • Review results on our server: encrypted, for about 72 hours.
  • A document’s text during a review: encrypted, only while the review runs (usually a few minutes, never more than an hour).
  • These copies are then deleted automatically, and they are never included in our backups or logs. Deleting a case or all data in the App doesn’t delete them sooner; they expire on their own.
  • Request records without content: 30 days. After that, only daily totals without device IDs remain.
  • IP addresses: 2 days in our device registration records; in our server logs, for a limited time for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • Your device record (random device ID, free allowance used, Premium status, offers used and subscription transaction IDs): as long as we run the Service, because we need it to apply your free allowance and Premium and to prevent abuse.
  • Records of acceptance (section 2): on your device until the device is erased; the copy in our usage analytics for up to 10 years, or longer if it is needed for a legal claim.
  • Database backups: they contain device records, request records without content and registration IP addresses, but no chats, documents, answers or reviews. We keep them for security and recovery: nightly backups for a limited time, then they are deleted; other copies, such as one made before a server update, until we delete them. So a backup can hold records already deleted from our live database, including request records older than 30 days and IP addresses older than 2 days.
  • Usage analytics: while we need them to understand and improve the App, and records of acceptance as described above. You can ask us to delete the analytics linked to your device (section 10).
  • Purchase records (Adapty): while you have a subscription, and afterwards as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal reasons.
  • Our AI providers: as described in section 3.

We may keep information longer if the law requires it, for example to respond to a valid legal order.

7. Sensitive Information

Legal questions often involve sensitive matters, such as health, money, immigration status, criminal matters or family. We process what you choose to share only to provide what you ask for: an answer, a review or a letter. We never use it for advertising, and we never send your chats or documents to our analytics or subscription providers.

8. Legal Requests and Privilege

Your conversations with Legal Mind are not protected by attorney–client privilege. We may disclose information we hold if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid subpoena or court order, or to protect someone’s safety. Because we keep your content on our server only briefly (about 24 hours for answers, about 72 hours for reviews), we usually have little to give. Our AI providers may receive such requests for data they hold.

9. Security

The App connects to our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Content stored on our server is encrypted (AES-GCM), and your cases are stored encrypted on your device. Access to our server is limited to us. No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires; because we don’t have your contact details, we will do so on our website and, where possible, in the App.

It doesn’t cancel Premium, reset your device ID or free allowance, change your analytics ID, or delete the records of acceptance (section 2), and copies on our server expire on their own (section 4).
  • Usage analytics. You can ask us to delete the usage analytics linked to your device (see “Requests to us”).
  • Requests to us. You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you (and for the names of the companies that currently receive it, as described in sections 3 and 6), or to correct or delete it, including in our analytics. Because there are no accounts, include the Order ID from Apple’s receipt if you bought Premium, or describe your device and when you used the App; even so, we may not be able to find all information linked to your device. Email contact.legalmind@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days (45 days where the law allows; we’ll tell you if we need more time). We may keep a minimal device record (free allowance used and Premium status) to prevent abuse of the free allowance, the records of acceptance, and records the law requires us to keep. We will pass a deletion request on to our AI providers where we can; content they hold is deleted on their own schedules (section 3).
  • Appeals and complaints. If we decline your request, reply “Appeal” and we will review it within 45 days. If you live in the US, you can also contact your state attorney general; in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, your data protection authority.
  • We won’t treat you differently for using these rights.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict some processing, and to receive your information in a portable format.
